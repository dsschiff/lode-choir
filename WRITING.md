# Lode Choir Writing Guide

## Voice

Write restrained strange science fiction. Use concrete people, tools, places,
damage, and costs. Keep the moon and the Orison uncanny, but do not explain an
ordinary game action with a metaphor.

## Rules

- Mechanical copy names the action and exact result first.
- Use one strong image at most in a story beat.
- Give Mara, Tamsin, Orin, and Sable distinct observations instead of generic
  speeches about truth, sacrifice, destiny, or burden.
- Prefer short declarative sentences. Avoid rhetorical fragments, stacked
  adjectives, abstract oppositions, and "not X but Y" constructions.
- Use one term for each thing: expedition for the full run, mission for one
  shift, room for a citadel module, staff for a room assignment, and deploy for
  resolving a mission.
- Preserve established facts and consequences. Atmosphere may never hide a
  cost, requirement, or outcome.
